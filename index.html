03 / FORMULA

Microfluidic technology

The serum features blue and clear micro-bubbles. CHANEL explains that the blue bubbles contain camellia oleo-extract, while the clear bubbles contain camellia oil. The micro-bubbles preserve the ingredients until application.

04 / APPLICATION

How to use

CHANEL recommends applying the serum morning and evening after cleansing. Dab a drop onto five or six areas of the face and neck, smooth outwards, and use gentle tapping movements with the fingertips.
